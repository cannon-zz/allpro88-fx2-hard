	terminated in a 40-pin connector, 4 of the pins over-hang the end
	of the 2-row 36-pin header.  1 of the 4 over-hanging pins in the
	40-pin connector has the ribbon cable conductor corresponding to
	pin 1 of the D-SUB connector connected to it, the other three have

also:
	- /RESET <--> 10 kOhm resistor to GND
	- /WR <--> 10 kOhm resistor to +3.3 V
	- /RD <--> 10 kOhm resistor to +3.3 V

the pull-up and pull-down resistors hold the control lines in safe states while the microcontroller starts, until it's able to take control.

(*) optinonally an LED and current-limiting series resistor can be connected from +3.3 V to CTL2.  the firmware sets CTL2 = CTL0 & CTL1 (low if either /RD or /WR is low) so the LED will blink to indicate read/write activity.
